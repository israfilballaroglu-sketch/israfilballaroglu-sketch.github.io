User Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of data used by iSoSoft Video Fabrikasi (also called State6Lab Uploader, "the app"). The app is a private desktop tool used only by its operator to publish content to their own Facebook Page (STATE 6 / State6Lab) and their own Instagram account.

What data the app uses

The app only uses the access tokens needed to publish videos, titles and descriptions to the operator's own Facebook Page and Instagram account, plus the basic account and Page IDs required to do that. Tokens are stored locally on the operator's own computer. The app has no server, does not collect data about other people, and does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.

How to request deletion

Send an email to israfilballaroglu@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request". Please include the Facebook or Instagram account name the request relates to. Requests are processed within 30 days, and you will receive a confirmation by email once the data has been deleted.

Remove the app's access yourself

Facebook:

  1. Open Facebook and go to Settings & privacy > Settings.
  2. Select Apps and websites.
  3. Find iSoSoft Video Fabrikasi and choose Remove.

Instagram:

  1. Open Instagram and go to Settings and privacy.
  2. Select Website permissions > Apps and websites.
  3. Find iSoSoft Video Fabrikasi under Active and choose Remove.

Removing access immediately stops the app from using your account. Any tokens stored locally become invalid.
