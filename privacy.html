Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This policy explains how State6Lab Uploader ("the app") handles data. The app is a private desktop tool used only by its operator to manage their own YouTube channels.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data the app accesses

With your permission, the app accesses your YouTube channel ID, channel name and basic channel details, and it uploads videos, titles, descriptions and thumbnails that you choose to publish.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to upload your videos and confirm which channel is connected. OAuth tokens and channel details are stored locally on your own computer. The app has no server, does not collect analytics and does not sell, share or transfer your data to any third party.

Revoking access

You can remove the app's access at any time from your Google account connections page. Deleting the app's local token files also removes stored access on your computer.
